Abstract: Annotating remote sensing images (RSIs) poses a significant challenge, primarily due to its labor-intensive nature. However, real-world scenarios may lead to a mixture of out-of-distribution (OOD) samples and in-distribution (ID) samples within the unlabeled dataset. The effectiveness of our proposed method is substantiated through extensive experiments on two widely used remote sensing object detection datasets: DIOR and DOTA.
