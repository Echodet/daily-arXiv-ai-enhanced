Existing models face core challenges such as feature degradation of small objects, extreme scale variations, and intricate, dynamic backgrounds. Experimental results on the RSOD and DIOR datasets demonstrate that TDO-YOLO achieves mAP$_{50}$scores of 96.6% and 68.7%, representing improvements of 3.87% and 3.15%, respectively, over the baseline model YOLOv11.
